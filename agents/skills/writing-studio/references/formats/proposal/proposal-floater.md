# Proposal — Floater level

A few sentences to float an idea and test interest, written before any doc exists. Often a chat message or short email. Read with `proposal.md` (the base) for what each section means.

## Goal

Get a quick reaction: is this worth pursuing or writing up properly? You are not making the full case, you are opening the conversation.

## Sections to include

Only these, from the base section library, kept to a sentence or two each:

- **Recommendation + ask** — what you propose, why it matters in one line, and the specific ask or the question you want answered ("worth me writing this up?").
- **Problem (one line)** — the pain, with a number if you have one to hand. No formal baseline section.
- **Impact of doing nothing (one line, optional)** — only if it is the hook.

Everything else (alternatives, metrics table, cost/ROI breakdown, dependencies, risk register) is **out of scope** at this level. If the reply is "tell me more", graduate to one-pager or full.

## Depth and shape

- 2-5 sentences, or a few short lines. One screen, no headings needed.
- Lead with the ask or the idea; no preamble ("Hi all, hope you're well").
- Plain and direct. Still never fabricate a number (A9) — if you do not know it, say so.

## Example shape

> Proposing we spin up a dedicated on-call rotation for the billing service. It's currently paging the whole team ~5x/week and burning the two people who know it. Ask: your OK to pilot a 3-person rotation for a month. Worth a full write-up, or is this a quick yes?
